# Review archive

One-off scripts and pages written during earlier code reviews (the findings they checked are in
`review_findings.json` and `final_review.json`). They used to live in `test/`, but CI never ran them: the unit
tests are `test/t_*.js` (`node ci/unit.js`).

They are kept for reference only and aren't maintained: their paths to `src/` were updated when they moved here,
but many were written against the source of their time and may no longer pass. The `review_f32ov_*.html` pages are
old builds of the app.
