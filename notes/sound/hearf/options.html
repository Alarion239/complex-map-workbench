A quick way to choose. Pick (z − 1)/(z + 1) and press Visit on Today, then on 1 (choir), 2 and 3. Then pick z²/(z − 1) and play Loop: a double zero on 1, 2 and 4. Then the Slow sweep on 1 (cello) against Today, and Deform on 2 and 4. After each, ask: would I leave this on for five minutes? Does it make a zero, a pole and a winding number obvious without looking?

How the options were chosen and what each one should sound like: OPTIONS.md next to this page. Measured loudness, clicks and CPU: PROTOTYPE.md.
