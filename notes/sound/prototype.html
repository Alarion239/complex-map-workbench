Measurements: every sound rendered offline (no output), at volume 100 %

Every cue goes through its stereo panner at the centre, as in use. "LUFS-M" is the loudest 400 ms momentary loudness (BS.1770 K-weighting); "phone" is the same through a 4th-order high-pass at 550 Hz (a phone speaker); targets: detents −40 ± 2, keys −32 ± 2, events −30 ± 2, export and error −29 ± 1, phone loss ≤ 5 dB. Peak is in dBFS before the safety clipper (linear below −6 dBFS, ceiling −1.6 dBFS), so a peak under −6 dBFS means the clipper never touches the sound. "Start" is the largest sample in the first 0.25 ms after the onset as a fraction of the peak (an abrupt start would be near 1); "end" is the last sample before the source stops (exactly 0 means no step). "HF" is the energy above 14 kHz relative to the total: a click spreads energy up there.
