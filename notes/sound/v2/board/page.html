Compare the sounds: A (in the app now) and B (the new palette)

Everything on this page plays through the palette chosen at the top. A is the app's engine as shipped (src/sound.js of v15). B is the proposal: struck and bowed materials, a new variation on every play, a speed-following slider sound. The two are matched in loudness cue by cue, so you can judge timbre rather than volume. Laptop speakers or headphones at a moderate level.

Try the slider below in A, then in B. Then hover the plane with the pointer.

1. The Deformation (the slider that "cracks")

Drag t like in the app. A ticks every 0.05 of t. B breathes with how fast you drag, rings a wooden note at t = 0, ¼, ½, ¾, 1, and is silent when your hand stops. Press play to hear the start, the motion and the arrival.

2. Hear the function

Hover: the tone's pitch class is arg F, so circling a zero climbs. Near a zero or a pole it rings its order (white dots: zeros, black: poles). Press and draw a loop: a bell for each full turn of arg F, then the winding number when you let go. Rest the pointer: in B the tone starts to sing (vibrato); in A it holds still.
