Guarantees

Drawing on the globes (item 8, phase P3b)

  1. X Sphere is the z-plane on the Riemann sphere (0 at the south pole, ∞ at the north). The globe is drawn with the conjugate lift, so it is not a mirror image of the plane: a half-plane lies to the left of its arrow, arcs and sectors turn counter-clockwise, the Tiles F reads correctly. Tools draw on it and store ordinary planar shapes. The graticule is the snap net: meridians every 15°, parallels |z| = ⅛ … 8.
  2. A Disc on the sphere is a cap (press at its centre on the sphere, drag the rim). Here the rim has snapped through ∞ (the accent ring at the north pole), so the cap is stored as the half-plane Im z > 1.5; the readout says so, with the kind tag, while dragging. Dragged further, ∞ falls inside and it becomes the complement of a disc. Handles: the cap's centre on the sphere (round) and its rim point (square).
  3. The disc |z − 0.6 − 1.2i| < 0.7 drawn earlier, exact on the globe (per-pixel test at stage 0: no hole near ∞ for unbounded shapes).
  4. Y Sphere shows the images on the w-sphere, exact through F−1: the half-plane's image is a disc whose rim passes through F(∞) = 1. Targets are drawn here the same way. The hint follows the tool (here Disc); the globe turns with Space-drag, the middle button or two fingers in every tool.
  5. The draft's image follows on Y as you drag (both panes draw the same shape list).
  6. Item 5, empty case: no targets yet, so the w-plane group shows "No targets yet. Draw one in the w-plane.", the same form as the shapes' note. Annulus, Sector and Polar grid are dimmed on globes (tooltip "Draw in Plane"): built around a planar centre they would not look concentric with a Disc drawn at the same press.
