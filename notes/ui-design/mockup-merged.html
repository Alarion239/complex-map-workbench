Key 1. Left pane = X (Plane | Sphere): the shapes. Right pane = Y (Plane | Sphere): the images and the targets. Each pane head keeps only its own Plane | Sphere switch; the morph button left the right head (it is the top bar's Deformation toggle now).

2 · Phase (one wide panel)

Key 2. The phase portrait of F over X, full width, Plane or Sphere (the phase sphere) in its head. Shapes outlined, target preimages drawn, the colour wheel with its w-plane key switch, and the style strip in the options strip.

3 · Landscape (one wide panel)

Key 3. The 3D graph of F over X: height, one colouring control (Tiles · Plain: the phase colours the relief can paint; Plaster), sheets and the probe in its options strip. No surface switch in its head (a landscape is its own surface).

4 · X → Y + Deformation (the two panes merge into one plane)

X drawn as a grey outline, Ht(X) filled with the pattern, live paths t ↦ Ht(z) of the morph figure's sample points (chosen off the frame, cached, never re-chosen during a scrub). The transport is docked at the panel's bottom, under the plot (it no longer covers the picture). Turning Deformation off splits the panes again.
